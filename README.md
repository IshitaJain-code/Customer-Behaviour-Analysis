
# Customer Behaviour Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze customer purchasing patterns, revenue performance, subscription behaviour, purchase frequency, and customer experience.

The project transforms raw customer data into meaningful business insights through data analysis, SQL querying, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer behaviour and identify patterns related to:

- Revenue and category performance
- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Subscription adoption
- Purchase frequency
- Customer satisfaction
- Shipping experience
- Customer demographics

The final output is an interactive Power BI dashboard that allows users to explore customer behaviour using filters for gender, age group, category, shipping type, and subscription status.

## 💰 2. Revenue Performance

This section focuses on the major drivers of revenue.

### Revenue by Category

The analysis shows that:

* Clothing generates approximately **$104K**
* Accessories generate approximately **$74K**
* Footwear generates approximately **$36K**
* Outerwear generates approximately **$19K**

Clothing is therefore the largest revenue-generating category in the dataset.

### Revenue by Age Group

Revenue is compared across:

* Young Adults
* Middle Aged
* Adults
* Seniors

This helps identify which customer age groups contribute most to overall revenue.

### Average Purchase by Category

Average purchase amounts are compared across product categories to understand differences in customer spending behaviour.

## 👥 3. Customer Behaviour

This section focuses on how customers purchase and interact with the business.

### Subscribers vs Non-Subscribers

The average purchase amount is compared between subscribed and non-subscribed customers.

The dashboard shows approximately:

* **Non-Subscribers:** $59
* **Subscribers:** $60

The difference is relatively small, suggesting that subscription status is not associated with a substantial difference in average purchase value in this dataset.

### Customers by Purchase Frequency

Customer purchasing behaviour is analyzed across different frequency categories:

* Every 3 Months
* Annually
* Quarterly
* Monthly
* Bi-Weekly
* Fortnightly
* Weekly

This provides an overview of how frequently customers make purchases.

## ⭐ 4. Customer Experience

This section focuses on customer satisfaction across different shipping methods.

### Average Rating by Shipping Type

Average customer ratings are compared across:

* Standard
* Express
* 2-Day Shipping
* Next Day Air
* Free Shipping
* Store Pickup

Ratings remain within a relatively narrow range of approximately **3.70–3.82**, indicating relatively consistent customer satisfaction across shipping methods.

## 🔎 Key Insights

### 1. Clothing is the largest revenue contributor

Clothing generates approximately **$104K**, making it the highest-revenue product category.

### 2. Accessories are the second-largest revenue contributor

Accessories generate approximately **$74K**, followed by Footwear and Outerwear.

### 3. Subscribers and non-subscribers have similar average purchase values

The average purchase amount is approximately **$59 for non-subscribers** and **$60 for subscribers**.

This indicates only a small difference in average transaction value between the two groups.

### 4. Subscription adoption is 27%

Approximately **27% of customers are subscribed**, indicating that the majority of customers are currently non-subscribers.

### 5. Customer ratings are relatively consistent across shipping methods

Average ratings range approximately from **3.70 to 3.82** across the different shipping options.
